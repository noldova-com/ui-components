# Word wrap

**Scope:** The kit's word wrap: its default geometry, the text role it uses, and its surface and behavior.

The themes, the tokens and the rules every control shares are in [KIT.md](../KIT.md), whose [Controls](../KIT.md#controls) section says how this specification reads.

## Default geometry

Icon-button geometry; 1rem wrap glyph.

## Text role

Accessible name Word wrap.

## Surface and behavior

Initially off, so code keeps its own shape; precedes Copy; pressed state exposed and filled as hover is; Enter/Space activate.
