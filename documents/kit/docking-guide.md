# Docking guide

**Scope:** The kit's docking guide: its default geometry, the text role it uses, and its surface and behavior.

The themes, the tokens and the rules every control shares are in [KIT.md](../KIT.md), whose [Controls](../KIT.md#controls) section says how this specification reads.

## Default geometry

2.5rem square around a 1.5rem glyph; small radius; a group's center target and split arrows share a medium-radius plate with 0.125rem gaps, which the owner places clear of the side guides.

## Text role

Accessible action name.

## Surface and behavior

Raised surface; the owner shows the guides while a view's tab is dragged; primary-button fill and text for the chosen target.
The owner previews the chosen target's landing area with the docking-preview fill, a 1px docking-preview border and the large radius.
