# Message

**Scope:** The kit's message: its default geometry, the text role it uses, and its surface and behavior.

The themes, the tokens and the rules every control shares are in [KIT.md](../KIT.md), whose [Controls](../KIT.md#controls) section says how this specification reads.

## Default geometry

0.5rem by 0.75rem padding, no top padding on a continuation; the avatar, at its normal size, in a gutter 0.75rem before the text; 0.5rem between header, blocks, each Show more and status; a 3px start bar; the Copy action 0.25rem from the top and 0.5rem from the end, and the header ends clear of it; code blocks up to 32rem high, then scrolling inside below their header; each run of text and code collapsed at the `message-collapsed-height` token, 24rem, or the height the app gives, fading out over its last 1.5rem.
A bubble, the person's own message, drops the avatar, the gutter and the padding at the end side: its content sits at the end edge, at most 70% of the message's width, padding included, with 0.75rem by 1rem padding, the medium radius and the message line height, 1.6; 0.25rem below it, at the end side, the Copy action and then the time, 0.5rem apart, then any status.
The column layout, `MessageLayout.Column`, for a conversation read as one column: 1.5rem above each message and no other padding or start bar; a header of the small avatar, the author's name and the app's header content, centered on one line, 0.75rem apart, wrapping with 0.25rem between lines, at the panel size, as is the time; 0.5rem between the header, the blocks and the footer, all on the message's start edge; the footer holds the app's actions and then Copy, 0.25rem apart, then the time 0.5rem after them; an interim answer 0.75rem after a 2px start border; the message line height, 1.6. A person's message in a column is the bubble, with its footer under it.

## Text role

Message, at the message line height, 1.6, in every layout, as a Markdown view on its own is set; the author at the emphasis weight in `text-strong`, in the column layout in the header's color; time, sizes and status in the label role, muted.

## Surface and behavior

The kit's `nol-message`, one row of a feed, named by a hidden label of its author, date and time, ending in "selected" when it is selected.
The avatar is the kit's [avatar](avatar.md), decorative beside the author's name, filled in the author's `avatarColor` when the app gives one, or else in the hue of the author's color, one of the eight author colors, or else the default hue; a continuation hides the avatar and the header.
An app that gives an `authorDescription`, such as an agent's model, has the author's name and avatar show the kit's [tooltip](tooltip.md) on hover, the name as its title and the description in muted text below it, and the name takes a focus stop that shows it too, while the avatar stays out of the tab order; without one, neither shows a tooltip nor takes focus.
The time reads like 2 minutes ago, or as the time of day, like 14:05, when the app asks for the clock, in the app's locale, with the date and time of day as its tooltip and its name, and a focus stop; with `twentyFourHour` on, every time of day it writes, its tooltip and name included, is on a 24-hour clock, like 09:05 and 16:01, whatever the locale's own clock; an app that gives its own formats with `provideTimeFormats` has the time of day written by its `clock` format and the tooltip's and name's date and time by its `full` one, each in place of the kit's and of `twentyFourHour`, redrawn when the formats change, while the relative time stays the kit's own; one clock moves every shown time each 30 seconds and stops when none is shown.
The app lays out the person's own message as a bubble on the `raised` surface, outlined in forced colors, with no avatar or author line; its Copy action and time show as the Copy action does, fading in and out over 120ms, or at once when reduced motion is preferred.
Text is Markdown once the app adds `provideMarkdown()` from `@noldova/ui.components.angular/markdown`; without it, and until the parser has loaded, each text block shows as written, plain, with its Markdown marks.
Markdown is drawn from its parsed tokens as elements, never as HTML: raw HTML stays text, an image shows its alt text unless the app gives an `ng-template nolMessageImage`, a single line break starts a new line as a hard break does, and only http, https and mailto links become link-colored, underlined links that the owner opens, with links whose scheme the owner names in `linkSchemes` (a `MarkdownLinkSchemes`, which refuses `http`, `https`, `mailto`, `mention`, `file`, `javascript`, `data` and `vbscript`) going to `linkActivate` like them, Enter with Ctrl or Cmd included, each also to `linkOpen` with how it was opened, plain by a click or Enter, modified with Ctrl or Cmd, or middle by a middle click, and to `linkMenu` on a right-click, the ContextMenu key or Shift+F10, whose default the owner cancels in its handler, before it returns, when it shows its own menu, and the message then asks for no menu of its own, one menu for each press, and links to a file's path too when the owner turns on `fileLinks`, which go to `fileActivate`, as do links to a `file:` URL with no host or `localhost`, which go as the path they name, decoded, with no slash before a Windows drive letter, which is in upper case on every system, and with their query and fragment, so `file:///C:/a.ts#L3` goes as `C:/a.ts#L3`, and otherwise show as written, start with the [file badge](file-badge.md) for the path without a `#` or `?` part after it (in a path, one written as is in its last segment), or with its folder mark when the owner's `folderLinks` holds the path as `fileActivate` gives it, 1rem, 0.25rem before the text and centered on its first line, and never wrap away from the text or enter the name, and otherwise show their text plain; with `shellRuns` on, each code block tagged as a shell, in the text at any depth or one of the message's own, offers [Run](code-block.md#surface-and-behavior), which goes to `shellRun`, and Run stays hidden while the message or view streams, so code still being written is never run, and never shows in the text on a block whose fence never closed, as a reply cut off mid-block leaves it; headings at the text's size and the emphasis weight, and bold text at the emphasis weight, in the strong text color; a heading among the text's own blocks, unless it is the first, sits 0.75rem further from the block before it than the usual gap, and one in a quote or a list item keeps the usual gap; quotes muted behind a 3px border; a task item shows the [plan](plan.md)'s 1rem marks in its marker space, 0.25rem from the text and centered on its first line, in the muted text color (`CanvasText` under forced colors), a filled check circle when done and a dashed circle when not, named Done or Not done; tables scroll sideways inside themselves.
The message and the Markdown view emit `filePathsChange` with the paths of the file links they show, in `fileActivate`'s form, each once and in the order of the text, whenever that list changes, while a reply streams too, so the owner checks which of them are folders without reading the Markdown again. The list is empty while `fileLinks` is off, so turning it off emits an empty list once, and nothing is emitted while the list stays empty. A message's list holds its own text's links, not those of a Markdown view the app puts in a slot, which takes no `folderLinks` from the message either.
Math is drawn by KaTeX in the text color, loaded on the first formula: `\( … \)` and `$ … $` in the line, `\[ … \]` and `$$ … $$` centered on a line of their own, scrolling sideways when wider than the text; a single `$` opens only before a character that isn't a space and closes only after one, never before a digit, so `$5 and $10` stays text, a `$` beside another, as in a `$$` that never closed, opens nothing, and math in code stays text.
Display math on lines of its own opens a line with `$$` or `\[` and ends at the end of the line its closing delimiter is on, with no blank line between; a closing delimiter anywhere else leaves the lines to the paragraph, a line indented as code stays code, and a link's text never ends inside a formula.
While a reply streams, KaTeX loads as soon as a formula opens, so the formula is drawn as it closes and the reply's height changes once.
A formula KaTeX can't read, one still being written while the text streams, and any formula before KaTeX has loaded show their source as written, delimiters included; Copy copies the source.
With `math` off, which an app sets for a person's own message, whose `$$` is often a shell's, the text has no math: its delimiters read as the rest of the text does, so `kill $$` shows as written, and KaTeX never loads for it; a reply leaves `math` on.
An app draws a text's images with an `ng-template nolMessageImage` in `nol-message` or `nol-markdown-view`, which takes `{ src, alt, title }` (`title` null when the image has none) in place of each image, inline where the image was, in an inline block no wider than the block it sits in, whose children are no wider than the inline block, and a markdown view with no template of its own uses the template of the message or view it sits in; the kit draws nothing for an image and never fetches its `src`, which is any address the text wrote, percent-encoded, for the app to check before it shows anything. A streamed text keeps what the template drew while it grows, an image in inline code or a code block stays text, and an image written by reference takes a definition checked as a link's, so only an http, https or mailto address defines it.

With `shellRuns` on, `shellRunStates`, a map from a code block's index to its `ShellRunState`, gives each [Run](code-block.md#surface-and-behavior)'s state, Idle for a block it doesn't name. The index counts the message's code blocks from 0 in reading order, across its texts, the blocks nested in their lists and quotes, and its own code blocks, and it holds while a reply streams, since streaming only adds blocks after it.
An app draws its own content under a code block with an `ng-template nolShellRunOutput` in `nol-message` or `nol-markdown-view`, which takes `{ $implicit: block, block }` and is drawn under each block whose index `shellRunOutputs` holds, a block gap below it, across the text column from start to end whatever the block's indent in a list or quote, in either direction, following the column's width; while no output shows, the layout is as without it. A reply's reveal passes over the output, as over any element marked `data-nol-reveal-skip`: it counts nothing in it, never hides it and never cuts its text or what the app adds into it, and an element past the reveal that holds it, such as a list item or a quote, has what is around it hidden rather than itself. A markdown view outside a message counts its own blocks and takes its own states, outputs and template.
An app that lays out its own message draws its text with `nol-markdown-view` from `@noldova/ui.components.angular/markdown`, as the message does, which also marks each `@name` of its `mentions`, the mention field's candidates: in the `mention` color at the emphasis weight, an unavailable candidate's at half opacity, in `CanvasText` under forced colors, and never in code; a link to `mention:<id>`, as the [mention field](mention-field.md#value)'s value writes a chip, shows its text as written in that look when a candidate has the id, and as plain text when none has. With `mentionNames` off, on the message or the view, only those links are marked and a candidate's name elsewhere shows as written, so an app marks only the places it names, such as a name a reply hands off to and not one it only quotes.
In a column every message has its header, a continuation included, unless the app turns `header` off, and a message by the app itself, a `System` author, has no avatar and keeps its text in the text color; an unavailable author's avatar takes its unavailable look while the name shows at half opacity; the header and an interim answer are muted, and take the text color when the message is selected.
A message in a column never collapses behind Show more and shows no status row of its own: the app shows its state in its header content, such as a status chip, and its working row in a slot after the last block; its actions and time show as a bubble's do.
The app adds its own actions with `nolMessageAction` on an icon button inside the message, before Copy, in the same toolbar that the arrow keys, Home and End move through.
The app adds content after the time with `nolMessageFooterEnd` on an element inside the message, such as a button that shows a reply's stats or a running reply's Stop, in every status, streaming included.
In the column and bubble layouts it fills the rest of the footer's row after the time: an item placed `AfterTime`, the default of `nolMessageFooterEndPlacement`, starts 0.25rem after the time, and an item placed `End` ends at the footer's end edge; each is centered on the time's line, and when the row is narrow the items stay after the time rather than wrapping before it.
In the row layout, whose time sits in the header, it follows Copy, 0.25rem after it, in the actions' box at the message's top end, and the header keeps clear of the whole box.
While it holds an item placed `End`, the footer, or the row layout's actions' box, shows without hover or focus, with its actions and time; one with only `AfterTime` items shows and hides with the actions as before.
Tab reaches a control in it after the time; it isn't part of the actions toolbar, so the arrow keys don't reach it. A message without it keeps its footer as before.
Copy, named Copy message, copies the text the app gives in `copyText`, or else the message's text blocks, joined by blank lines, leaving out interim answers, and shows Copied as the kit's [copy](copy.md) does.
A selected message in a column takes the selected fill and a 3px accent bar inside its start edge, and a 2px `Highlight` outline inside it under forced colors.
An app that takes the person to one message, such as a search hit, sets its `flashStart` to that time: from then the message fades a tint of 18% accent from its own shape over 1.6 seconds, easing out, once for each start, and null stops it. The shape is the bubble's content with its corners, its tint mixed over the `raised` surface; in a column, a box around the message's header and content with a person's message's corners and padding, 0.75rem above and below them and 1rem past each side, behind them so nothing in the message moves, and ending 0.75rem below the content whether or not the footer row is there, its tint laid over whatever is under it, such as the selected fill; a message selected in the `Raised` look that isn't a bubble, in a column as in a row, flashes on its raised fill's own box instead, with its large corners, its tint mixed over the `raised` surface; or the whole row otherwise, its tint mixed over the selected fill when the row is selected. The app keeps the start, so a message drawn again while it flashes, such as a recycled row, or a hidden message shown again, goes on with the flash where it was, and a new start plays it again even when the row wasn't drawn in between. Under forced colors the flash is a 2px `Highlight` outline inside the same shape that fades instead, just inside a selected message's own outline in the `Raised` look; when reduced motion is preferred, the tint or outline holds for the 1.6 seconds and then goes at once. The flash never changes the message's label or what it announces.
A streaming message shows a spinner with Writing and never collapses, and the app marks its feed busy.
While it streams, its last text block, unless it is interim, reveals a little at a time, in bursts about 50 ms apart and every frame once it ends (none under reduced motion), holding back an unfinished end, an image or a formula appearing whole, and keeps its elements when a slot comes before it, such as an activity row; only the last block grows, so an earlier text block shows whole.
A text view whose text is replaced rather than grown, as when a text block before it goes and the next block's text moves into its place, shows the new text whole, and reveals by frames only what is then added to it.
A text block that a later step makes interim, or that a later block leaves no longer last, while it is still revealing shows its unfinished end, and goes on revealing at the same pace until it has caught up or the message ends, when the rest catches up as an ended reply's does; one that is interim when it arrives shows whole.
A text block past the text blocks the message is first drawn with reveals the same way, from nothing, also when only a slot, such as a working row, was drawn before it; a view that has already drawn text, though, never reveals from nothing, so a block that moves into it shows whole.
The part not yet shown is cut from the text, in every look, forced colors included, as before 0.0.50: the text is laid out at the size of what it shows, so a block, such as a code block, or an inline code's chip grows with the reveal, and a code's lines past the one the reveal reaches are written empty. A screen reader and Copy see the text shown so far while a reply reveals. While a selection, not just a caret, reaches into a reply that is revealing, the reply shows all that has arrived at once, so the selection draws and copies only text the reader sees; once the selection goes, what arrives next reveals by frames again.
A cancelled message shows a block glyph with Cancelled; a failed one an error-colored glyph and its error, with a secondary Retry button only while its host listens to `retry`, so a host that cannot retry shows no control that does nothing.
A reply that streamed in view stays expanded once it completes, with Show less when it is longer than the collapsed height.
Focus moving to a part below a collapsed run's cut, or into its fade, expands the run.
A streamed message announces, politely, its author and that the reply is complete, or failed with its error, never its text, once for its key.
Attachments and slots never collapse; attachments are bordered buttons on the panel surface with a preview, or the [file badge](file-badge.md) for the name, 1rem, 0.5rem before the text and centered on the name's line, then the name and the type and size, and the kit's tooltip with the attachment's `tooltip` text, such as its full path, when it has one, and none when it doesn't or the text is empty.
An attachment given a picture shows as that picture instead, in its place among the blocks, in a bubble, a column or a row: a button named by the file's name, with the same tooltip, holding the image without a caption, as wide as the image but never wider than the content nor taller than `message-picture-height`, 24rem, at the image's aspect ratio, so its space is kept before the image loads and the feed doesn't move, and at least the 24px pointer target each way, where a small or thin image shows scaled down and centered; a 1px border in the card border color drawn over the image's edge, CanvasText under forced colors, medium corners, the kit's focus ring, a zoom-in cursor and, under forced colors, the dashed highlight outline on hover while it isn't focused, as on an attachment's button; choosing it opens the attachment as its button does. The picture needs the image's own size, so an app that has an image without one decodes it first, or shows it as a file's button.
A selected message takes the selected fill and an accent start bar, the system's highlight in forced colors, where an unselected message's bar is the `Canvas` ground so none shows, and its muted time, status, empty text, system text and quotes take the text color; attachment sizes stay muted on the attachment's own fill.
With `selectionLook` set to `Raised`, for a list whose selected item sits on the raised background, such as a view of a chat's plans, a selected message takes the raised fill, `--nol-raised`, with large corners and no bar, in any layout, keeps its size and its content's place, and is outlined in `Highlight` 2px inside its edges under forced colors; its name still ends in "selected". `Bar`, the default, is the look above.
A column message has no inline padding of its own, so an app that sets column messages with the `Raised` look pads each one itself, to keep its content off the raised fill's edges, and an app leaves room where a column message's flash reaches past its box: 1rem past each side, and 0.75rem below it when its footer is off.
With `footer` off, a column message draws no footer row, so no time or footer-end content, and ends at its content; its header holds its actions instead, the app's and then Copy, in the same toolbar, 0.75rem after the app's header content and centered on the header's line, which they don't make taller; when that line has no room left for them, about 14rem for a header with the small avatar, a short name and one chip, they take a row of their own at the header's start, under its content, and the header grows by that row; they come before the message's content, so Tab and a screen reader reach them, Copy included, before the message's text; they show as the footer does, and Copy copies, says Copied and copies `copyText` as it does there. A person's message in a column, the bubble, has no header, so it shows no actions with `footer` off. The row and bubble layouts always keep their footer.
With `header` off, a column message draws no header row, so no avatar, name or header content, and starts at its content, for an app that shows its author inside the content, such as a plan card's own header; with `footer` also off, its actions show at its top end, 0.25rem from its top and 0.5rem from its end, as the row layout's do. They stand over the content's first line, which the message leaves at its full width, so the app keeps that corner clear, as a plan card pads its header's end by the actions' width. The app sets how far below the message's top edge these actions stand with `actionsTop`, a CSS length, such as one that centers them on a plan card's header; null, the default, keeps them 0.25rem from the top, and it moves the row layout's actions the same way, while actions in a header or a footer row keep their place. It sets how far from the message's end edge they stand with `actionsEnd`, a CSS length, such as one that gives Copy a plan card's header gap; null, the default, keeps them 0.5rem from the end, and it moves the same actions. The row layout always keeps its header, and a person's bubble has none either way.
The Copy action shows on hover, while the message or its row has focus, while a control in its actions, footer or tools is expanded, such as the trigger of an open popover, which takes the focus out of the message, and always without hover; Escape from a control inside returns to the row, or to the feed when the row is a feed's article; a right click, the ContextMenu key or Shift+F10 asks the app to open its context menu, at the pointer or below the focused part, for the focused part, with the id of the attachment it was on, as its picture or its button, so the app's menu can open, copy or save that file, and the message stops that event, so a trigger around it opens once.
Text stays selectable.
