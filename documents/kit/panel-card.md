# Panel card

**Scope:** The kit's panel card: its default geometry, the text role it uses, and its surface and behavior.

The themes, the tokens and the rules every control shares are in [KIT.md](../KIT.md), whose [Controls](../KIT.md#controls) section says how this specification reads.

## Default geometry

1px border; large radius.

## Text role

Inherited.

## Surface and behavior

The panel surface, or the shell surface when the owner asks for it.
