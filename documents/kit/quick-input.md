# Quick input (search)

**Scope:** The kit's quick input: its default geometry, the text role it uses, and its surface and behavior.

The themes, the tokens and the rules every control shares are in [KIT.md](../KIT.md), whose [Controls](../KIT.md#controls) section says how this specification reads.

## Default geometry

Preferred width `quick-input-width`, 40rem, capped at the nearest size container around it less 1rem, or else at the viewport less 1rem: in a global overlay, whose wrapper is a size container, that is the overlay bounds, while a connected overlay leaves it capped at the viewport; `quick-input-margin`, 0.25rem, top margin; padding `quick-input-padding`, 0.375rem, which the field takes at its top and sides, the filters and the footer at their sides, the footer at its bottom, and the results inside their scrolling area at their sides and bottom, so the results fill the surface to its end and bottom edges and their scrollbar runs along the surface's end edge to its bottom, so an app sets that padding through `--nol-quick-input-padding`, never on the host, where it would inset the scrollbar again; the field, the filters, the results and the footer `quick-input-gap`, 0.375rem, apart; a `quick-input-field-height`, 1.875rem, field with `quick-input-field-padding`, 0.5rem, side padding; results at most `quick-input-list-height`, 60% of the viewport's height, or ten rows when that is more, so the one search's nine results in five groups show whole in an 800px-high window, within the space the owner gives it, with the active result kept above their bottom padding; `quick-input-row-height`, 1.75rem, rows with `quick-input-row-padding`, 0.625rem, side padding and the same gap between their parts, their fill the whole row with a small radius, so a result without an icon keeps the icon's space and every title starts at the same start edge; a group's heading on its own row above its results, 0.5rem above and 0.25rem below its text, whose line is `quick-input-label-line`, the font's own line height, as the footer's is, starting at their icons' start edge, and `quick-input-gap` between two groups, with no line; with an icon, a `quick-input-glyph`, 0.875rem, glyph inside the field one field padding from its border, and the query one field padding after it; while there are no results, an empty text one result row high in their place, starting at the query's start edge; the owner's filters 0.125rem in from the field's edges, 0.25rem apart, and its footer's hints from the results' icons' start edge, 1rem apart, 0.125rem above and below them; large radius.

## Text role

Panel; details, group headings and the footer small; matches and group headings semibold; details, keys, group headings, the footer, the field's glyph and the empty text muted; a key in a key cap of `quick-input-key-text`, 0.6875rem, on the `quick-input-label-line` line, with the hover radius and a `--nol-quick-input-key-border` border.

## Surface and behavior

Quick-input surface, `--nol-menu-border` border and extra-large shadow; non-modal, placed by the owner, scrolling its results under the thin scrollbar.
The field is a combobox over a listbox, focused on opening, with the first result active; the owner filters the results as the person types, which makes the first result active again; Up, Down, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down move the active result, and a change to the results keeps it where the person put it while it is listed, until the query changes, and otherwise makes the first result active; the active result stays in view by scrolling the results alone, never the page around them, and uses the selected fill, Enter or a click chooses it, and Escape asks the owner to close it.
The owner closes it on Escape or a click outside, returning focus to where it was.
With Shift, Ctrl, Alt or Meta held, those keys go to the field instead, so Shift+Home selects the query.
Each result shows its icon, its title, a detail and its key. A result with a section starts a group, which runs to the next result with a section and is a `group` labelled by the section, shown as the group's heading, which also describes the group's first result, for a screen reader that doesn't read a group's label when the active result moves into it; results before the first section are in a group without a heading. Moving to a group's first result shows its heading too, so Home shows the first heading again.
A result too long for its row, as at 200% zoom, cuts its detail first, ending it with an ellipsis, and its title only once the detail is gone, keeping both in its name; once the keys move or filter the results, the active result, while cut short, shows its title and detail in a truncation tooltip outside the results; each move or filter shows it afresh, the first Escape hides it, so the second asks the owner to close the search, and it goes once the field loses focus.
A match is marked where it falls, in the title or the detail, in the accent color at the strong weight, with the text unchanged around it; the result count is announced as a status.
An app's search palette can show a glyph in the field, and a text in the results' place while there are none, such as a hint, a "Nothing found." or a failure, wrapping within the panel; it is announced instead of the result count, politely, or as an alert when the app marks it a failure, and while it shows, the field reports its list closed and controls nothing.
An owner can give a filters row under the field, each element it marks `nolQuickInputFilters`, such as one chip per filter, and a footer under the results, each element it marks `nolQuickInputFooter`, such as one hint per key; each row shows only when given.
Tab and Shift+Tab in the field go to the owner as `tabKey`, with the key's event, such as to move to the next filter: the owner that calls `preventDefault` on it keeps the focus in the field, and otherwise the focus moves on as usual.
Under forced colors, the active result's matches, key and key cap take the highlighted text on the highlight.
