# Card

**Scope:** The kit's card: its default geometry, the text role it uses, and its surface and behavior.

The themes, the tokens and the rules every control shares are in [KIT.md](../KIT.md), whose [Controls](../KIT.md#controls) section says how this specification reads.

## Default geometry

Automatic height; 0.75rem padding; medium radius; 1px border.

## Text role

Message.

## Surface and behavior

Raised surface; the kit's card; a long word wraps inside it rather than widen it.
