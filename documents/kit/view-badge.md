# View badge

**Scope:** The kit's view badge: its default geometry, the text role it uses, and its surface and behavior.

The themes, the tokens and the rules every control shares are in [KIT.md](../KIT.md), whose [Controls](../KIT.md#controls) section says how this specification reads.

## Default geometry

1rem high pill with 0.25rem side padding, at least 1rem wide; a 0.5rem dot without a count; 0.625rem text, at the emphasis weight.

## Text role

Hidden; its description joins the view's accessible name.

## Surface and behavior

Primary-button fill and text; a count up to 99, then 99+.
